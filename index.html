How the numbers work

Projections. Sleeper's weekly projections for weeks 6–18, re-scored with MVP FOOTBALL settings: full PPR, 6-pt passing TDs, +0.5 per TE catch, and expected 100/200-yd and 300/400-yd bonuses. Past games use actual stats with the same scoring.

Rankings. Value over replacement. Replacement level at each position is set by how many players the 12 teams actually start there (superflex and both flex spots included).

Matchups. Fantasy points each defense has allowed per game to each position in all games played so far this season, in your league's scoring. Matchup score runs 10 (toughest) to 100 (softest).

Trade value. Dynasty market values for 12-team superflex PPR (FantasyCalc). Values marked est are outside that feed's top 100 and estimated from DynastyProcess values on the same scale. 2nd and 3rd round pick values are estimates.

Trade grade. A player who would not start for his new team counts 30% less. Trade edge is value received divided by all value in the deal: 55%+ Strong Accept, 52–55% Lean Accept, 48–52% Fair, 45–48% Lean Pass, under 45% Strong Pass.

Season outlook. Each team’s weekly score is its best projected lineup for that week (byes included) plus random variation sized from this league’s actual scores so far this season. The rest of the regular season is played out thousands of times; playoff odds are the share of runs a team finishes in the top 6.

Playoff bracket. Sleeper\u2019s 6-team format: seeds 3v6 and 4v5 in Week 15, the 1 seed plays the 4/5 winner and the 2 seed the 3/6 winner in Week 16, final in Week 17. The bracket shown is the single most likely path; title odds come from playing out every simulated season, so they include the chance seeds land differently.

Waivers. A pickup counts as helping now only if he would make your best lineup. The suggested drop is the bench player with the least trade value and rest-of-season output.

Trade Finder. Tests every 1-for-1, 2-for-1 and 1-for-2 deal between your top players and 1st/2nd-round picks and each team's top players. It keeps deals graded 48–60% for you, so the other side is not asked to take a lopsided loss. Position rooms are ranked by your top 2 QB, 3 RB, 4 WR and 1 TE.

FAAB. Remaining waiver budget can be added to either side of a trade. Its value per dollar defaults to the median of this league\u2019s winning bids of $10 or more, divided into the bought player\u2019s trade value; change it in the Trade Analyzer. Trade Finder can use FAAB to even out deals that would otherwise land just outside the fair range.

2027 picks. Valued by where the original team is projected to finish in the season simulation, assuming the rookie draft goes in reverse order of final standings. Picks 1\u20134 are early, 5\u20138 mid, 9\u201312 late.

Estimated values. Players outside the market feed's top 100 are estimated from RotoBot's league-wide player scores where available (fit to market values), otherwise from DynastyProcess values.
